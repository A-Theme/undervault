Refund policy

Lanterns of the Undervault · last updated 10 October 2026

If the full game isn't for you, ask within 14 days of buying it and you get your money back. No reason needed.

Bought on the website

  • Email support@playundervault.com within 14 days of your purchase, with the email address you paid with or your order number from the receipt. You can also ask Link support, who handle orders for our payment provider, Stripe.
  • The refund goes back to the way you paid, through Stripe (Link sends you the refund receipt). Banks usually take 5 to 10 business days to show it.
  • After 14 days we still look at every request, for example if you were charged twice or the game never unlocked.

Bought in the Android app

  • Google Play handles these. Within 48 hours you can request a refund from Google Play yourself; see Google Play's refund help.
  • After that, email support@playundervault.com within 14 days of buying, with your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA), and we will refund it through Google Play.

What happens after a refund

The full game is locked again on that account. Your heroes and their progress are kept, and Book I stays free, so you can buy it again later and carry on.

Your rights

This policy adds to, and does not take away from, any rights you have under the consumer protection laws of where you live.
