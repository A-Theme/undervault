Emberfall was built on a lid. Three hundred years ago the Lantern Order chained something beneath the city, and five Wardstones hold the seal. Tonight the Last Lantern turned blue. You answered.

Every room is written prose. Every command is a word you type or a button you tap. And every sword, staff and suit of armor you find shows up on your pixel-art hero.

What is in it

  • Twelve classes: Warrior, Mage, Rogue, Cleric, Ranger, Necromancer, Druid, Brawler, Demon, Scion, Elementalist and Worg, each with its own skills, talents and titles.
  • A story in eight books. Four are here: eighty floors, twenty story bosses, and every book ends with a choice. Books V to VIII are on the way, starting with The Quiet Kingdom, and they come with the full game.
  • A story in eight books. Five are here: a hundred floors, twenty-five story bosses, and every book ends with a choice. Books VI to VIII are on the way, and they come with the full game.
  • Bosses that warn you: every boss shows you what it is about to do. Defend, Dodge, or break it with a skill or a stun, and watch for the feint.
  • Loot you can see: weapons and armor change how your hero looks, with legendaries, class sets and enchantments.
  • Side dungeons at four difficulty tiers, an endless Abyss with a leaderboard, and a daily dungeon.
  • Side challenges behind the walls: some walls open only to your class's own skill, and behind them wait chess in a drowned court, a tower defence in the snow, a forge trial, a race up a stair and more, each with four tiers and a prize of its own. And secrets, for the curious.
  • Friends: talk in the Common Room (a chatroom for everyone online, with the last day kept), race the same seed, play live together on the same floor, or form a party of four for the raids. No one free? The ghosts of your line fill the empty seats.
  • A legacy that outlasts your heroes: heirlooms that grow with whoever carries them, a family crypt that remembers every fallen Hardcore hero, and the Echo Hall of every keeper you have beaten.
  • Pets to tame and raise through ten forms, in five rarities; Hardcore mode, controller support, and it plays offline.
  • Combat sounds mixed from free-to-use (CC0) sounds by Kenney, artisticdude and rubberduck; the spells from CC0 recordings by lentikula (Basic Spell Impacts) and Julien Matthey.

Book V: The Quiet Kingdom

After the war, an envoy from Marrowgate waits in the Square, and Captain Varo sails you west. Marrowgate is a city where nobody speaks above a whisper, and under its cathedral is the fourth door. The thing behind it is not hungry, thirsty or cold. It is lonely, and it keeps every sound it hears.

  • Twenty floors and five keepers, from Mother Quiet on the docks to the Listener under the choir.
  • The quiet: every sound you make fills a noise bar, and something is listening.
  • Three factions of Marrowgate ask you to choose, one act at a time.
  • Three side challenges: the Whisper Wells, the Silent Duel and the Muffled Peal.
  • Two raids: the Hundred Hushed, and the Boy Who Stayed.

Where to play

The whole game plays on all of these. Sign in (Google or Discord) and your heroes and your purchase follow you from one to the next.

  • PC, Mac, Linux or Chromebook: open playundervault.com in any browser. To install it as an app, use Chrome or Edge and click the install icon in the address bar. Controllers work.
  • Steam Deck: press the Steam button, then Power, Switch to Desktop. Open the app menu at the bottom left, then System, Konsole, and paste curl -fsSL https://playundervault.com/steamdeck.sh | bash (Steam + X brings up the keyboard). When it says Done, double-click Return to Gaming Mode: the game is in your Library, under Non-Steam.
  • iPhone and iPad: open playundervault.com in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It runs full-screen and offline, like an app.
  • Android: open playundervault.com in Chrome, tap the menu (⋮), then Install app. Or get it on Google Play, or download undervault.apk.
