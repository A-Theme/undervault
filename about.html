Lanterns of the Undervault

A text dungeon crawler in the spirit of the old MUDs, with pixel-art heroes. Made by A-Theme.

Play it in your browser

Emberfall was built on a lid. Three hundred years ago the Lantern Order chained something beneath the city, and five Wardstones hold the seal. Tonight the Last Lantern turned blue. You answered.

Every room is written prose. Every command is a word you type or a button you tap. And every sword, staff and suit of armor you find shows up on your pixel-art hero.

What is in it

  • Twelve classes: Warrior, Mage, Rogue, Cleric, Ranger, Necromancer, Druid, Brawler, Demon, Scion, Elementalist and Worg, each with its own skills, talents and titles.
  • A story in four books so far: eighty floors, twenty story bosses, and every book ends with a choice.
  • Loot you can see: weapons and armor change how your hero looks, with legendaries, class sets and enchantments.
  • Side dungeons at four difficulty tiers, an endless Abyss with a leaderboard, and a daily dungeon.
  • Friends: race the same seed, play live together, or form a party of four for the raids.
  • 24 pets to tame, Hardcore mode, controller support, and it plays offline.
