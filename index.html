The Last Lantern is guttering

Three hundred years ago the Lantern Order chained something beneath Emberfall, and five Wardstones hold the seal. Tonight the Last Lantern turned blue. Sister Maren sent for anyone who can hold a weapon. You answered.

What waits below

Twelve classes

Warrior, Mage, Rogue, Cleric, Ranger, Necromancer, Druid, Brawler, Demon, Scion, Elementalist and Worg, each with its own skills, talents and titles.

A story in four books

Eighty floors, twenty story bosses, side dungeons at four tiers, and an endless Abyss. Every book ends with a choice.

Loot you can see

Every sword, staff and suit of armor shows up on your hero. Legendaries, class sets, enchantments, and chests that open with a flourish.

Friends and pets

Race the same seed, play live together or raid in a party of four. Tame and evolve 24 pets. Hardcore mode and a daily dungeon.

Install it

Install on this device

Android

  1. Open playundervault.com in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu (⋮), then Install app.

Or download the app: undervault.apk.

Google Play: coming soon.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open playundervault.com in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

It runs full-screen and offline, like an app.

Windows, Mac and Linux

  1. Open playundervault.com in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the install icon in the address bar.

Or just play it in any browser tab. Controllers work too.

Steam Deck

  1. Press the Steam button, then Power → Switch to Desktop.
  2. Open the app menu at the bottom left, then System → Konsole.
  3. Paste this and press Enter (Steam + X brings up the keyboard):
    curl -fsSL https://playundervault.com/steamdeck.sh | bash
  4. When it says Done, double-click Return to Gaming Mode on the desktop.
  5. In your Library, open the Non-Steam tab and start Lanterns of the Undervault.

The Deck's controls work in the game.
